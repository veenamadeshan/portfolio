Client Projects

Campaigns I wrote for brands across fintech, D2C and B2B. Open one to see the brief, what I wrote and why it works.

Eight video scripts that make bond details feel simple

Strategy Video scripts Statics Fintech

Brief

Get first-time investors to look at a bond’s issuer, tenure, payout and credit rating, not just its return, in time for Diwali.

What I wrote

Eight scripts of about 40 seconds each, in English and Hinglish, plus three static creatives. Each one opens with a worry viewers already have.

  • “Diwali is almost here, and my client still hasn’t paid me.”
    Opening line, Diwali payment script
  • “Yaar, return toh samajh aa raha hai… but paisa kab milega?”
    Opening line, Hinglish script

Why it works

Risk is stated plainly every time: a credit rating helps you assess risk, it doesn’t remove it.
